UrbanCart — A Custom Storefront Built To Replace A Slow, Templated Shopify Setup

Pixelync designed and built a fully custom e-commerce platform and inventory system for UrbanCart, a home-goods retailer that had outgrown off-the-shelf tooling.

ClientUrbanCart Home Goods
IndustryRetail / E-Commerce
Timeline14 Weeks
ServicesWeb Dev, Cloud, UI/UX

The Challenge

UrbanCart had scaled past what their template-based Shopify store could handle. Page load times were slow on mobile, inventory synced manually across two warehouses, and every custom feature request meant paying for a third-party app subscription — several of which conflicted with each other.

  • Checkout abandonment was climbing due to slow page speeds
  • Manual inventory reconciliation caused regular overselling
  • No control over checkout UX or promotional logic

The Solution

We built a custom storefront on a headless architecture, with a Node.js/PostgreSQL backend handling catalog, inventory and order logic, and a React frontend optimized for speed. A dedicated inventory service keeps stock levels in sync across both warehouses in real time.

  • Server-rendered React storefront with sub-2-second load times
  • Real-time inventory sync across both warehouse locations
  • Custom promotions engine replacing three paid Shopify apps
  • Stripe-powered checkout with saved payment methods

The Outcome

UrbanCart launched on the new platform with zero downtime during migration. Within the first full quarter, mobile conversion improved significantly and the retailer regained full control over their checkout experience and promotional strategy.
