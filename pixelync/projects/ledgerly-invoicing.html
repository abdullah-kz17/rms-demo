Ledgerly — Invoicing And Expense Tracking Built For Freelancers And Small Agencies

Pixelync designed and built Ledgerly from the ground up: a lightweight SaaS invoicing platform for freelancers and small agencies who found existing tools too bloated for their workflow.

ClientLedgerly Inc.
IndustrySaaS / FinTech
Timeline16 Weeks
ServicesWeb Dev, Cloud, UI/UX

The Challenge

Ledgerly's founder — a former freelance designer — was frustrated that every invoicing tool on the market was either too limited (spreadsheets) or built for large accounting teams with features freelancers never use. There was no lean option built specifically for solo operators and two-to-five-person agencies.

  • Existing tools buried simple invoicing behind enterprise accounting features
  • No clean way to track project expenses against a client budget
  • Manual chasing of overdue invoices ate hours every week

The Solution

We built Ledgerly as a focused Next.js application with a Prisma/PostgreSQL data layer, deployed on AWS. The dashboard centers on three things: fast invoice creation, per-project expense tracking, and automated payment follow-ups.

  • Invoice creation and sending in under 60 seconds
  • Per-project budget tracking with expense tagging
  • Automated payment reminders with configurable escalation
  • Stripe and bank transfer payment collection built in

The Outcome

Ledgerly launched with a working self-serve signup flow and onboarded its first cohort of freelance users within weeks of launch, with early users citing the automated reminders as the single feature that paid for the subscription.
