TableWise — A Reservation App Built For Independent Restaurants

Pixelync designed and built TableWise, a cross-platform booking and table management app that helps independent restaurants replace spreadsheets and phone-only reservations.

ClientTableWise Hospitality
IndustryRestaurants / Hospitality
Timeline10 Weeks
ServicesMobile Dev, UI/UX

The Challenge

TableWise's founding restaurant group managed reservations across a shared spreadsheet and a landline, which meant double-bookings during peak hours and no visibility into table turnover across their three locations.

  • Double-bookings during Friday and Saturday peak service
  • No shared visibility across the group's three locations
  • Guests had no way to book or manage reservations online

The Solution

We built a cross-platform app in Flutter for hosts and a lightweight web widget for guest-facing bookings, backed by Firebase for real-time table state. Stripe handles optional deposit collection for large parties.

  • Real-time table availability shared across all locations
  • Guest-facing booking widget embeddable on any website
  • Automated SMS confirmations and reminders
  • Deposit collection for parties of six or more

The Outcome

Within the first month, TableWise eliminated double-bookings entirely across all three locations and cut no-shows meaningfully thanks to automated reminders — freeing host staff to focus on the floor instead of the phone.
