FitPulse, A Coaching App For Independent Trainers

Pixelync built FitPulse, an app that lets independent trainers build workout plans, track client progress and message clients, all in one place.

ClientFitPulse Coaching
IndustryHealth & Fitness
Timeline13 Weeks
ServicesMobile Dev, Cloud, UI/UX

The Challenge

FitPulse's founder, an independent trainer, managed client workout plans across spreadsheets, PDFs and WhatsApp messages. There was no single place to track client progress or change plans quickly, and every new client meant more manual work.

  • Workout plans scattered across PDFs and chat messages
  • No easy way to track client progress over time
  • Manual scheduling and payment reminders eating hours every week

The Solution

We built FitPulse as an app for both trainers and clients, with Firebase keeping progress and notifications in sync in real time. Trainers build reusable workout templates and track client progress right in the app.

  • Drag-and-drop workout builder with reusable templates
  • Live progress tracking with charts clients can see
  • Messaging between trainer and client, right in the app
  • Automatic session reminders and repeat payment requests

The Outcome

FitPulse launched with its founding trainer's full client list moved over in the first two weeks, and clients stuck to their plans better once they could see their progress in real time.
