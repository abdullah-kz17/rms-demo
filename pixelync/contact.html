Getting Started

We build websites, mobile apps, cloud systems, and good design. We also give simple, honest tech advice. Most projects combine two or three of these, for example, a new app plus the cloud setup to run it.

We work best with founders and small teams who want to talk directly to the people building their product, not an account manager. Book a free call and we'll tell you honestly if we're a good match.

We can usually set up a call within a week of your first message, and send you a clear plan within two weeks after that.

Pricing & Timeline

Most projects cost between $5,000 for a small app and $50,000+ for bigger, longer builds. We give you a clear price after a free call, we don't work off a fixed price list.

A small app usually takes 8 to 14 weeks from start to launch. Bigger projects take longer. We'll always give you a realistic timeline, not just a best case.

Most projects are split into a deposit plus a few payments as we hit agreed milestones, so you're never paying far ahead of the work. Exact terms are set for each project before we begin.
