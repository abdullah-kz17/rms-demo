Ledgerly, Simple Invoicing For Freelancers And Small Agencies

Pixelync built Ledgerly from scratch, a simple invoicing and expense tool for freelancers and small agencies who found other tools too complicated.

ClientLedgerly Inc.
IndustrySaaS / FinTech
Timeline16 Weeks
ServicesWeb Dev, Cloud, UI/UX

The Challenge

Ledgerly's founder, a former freelance designer, was tired of every invoicing tool being either too basic (spreadsheets) or built for big accounting teams, full of features freelancers never use. There was nothing simple made for solo workers and small two-to-five-person agencies.

  • Existing tools hid simple invoicing behind complicated accounting features
  • No easy way to track project costs against a client's budget
  • Chasing late invoices by hand ate hours every week

The Solution

We built Ledgerly as a focused Next.js app with a Prisma and PostgreSQL database, hosted on AWS. The dashboard does three things well: fast invoices, expense tracking per project, and automatic payment reminders.

  • Create and send an invoice in under 60 seconds
  • Track spending per project with simple tags
  • Automatic payment reminders that get firmer over time
  • Built-in payment collection by card or bank transfer

The Outcome

Ledgerly launched with a simple sign-up flow and its first group of freelance users within weeks, with many saying the automatic reminders alone were worth paying for.
