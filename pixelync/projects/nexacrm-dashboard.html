NexaCRM, A Simple Sales Tool For Small Teams

Pixelync built NexaCRM, a simple sales tracking tool for small teams who found tools like Salesforce too complicated for a five-person team.

ClientNexaCRM Inc.
IndustrySaaS / Sales Tech
Timeline15 Weeks
ServicesWeb Dev, Cloud, UI/UX

The Challenge

NexaCRM's founder had used big sales tools at past jobs and found them too much for a small team, dozens of fields nobody uses, confusing permissions, and a long setup just to track deals and follow-ups.

  • Older tools took weeks to set up before a team could use them
  • Deal progress was scattered across spreadsheets and inboxes
  • No simple option made for teams under 10 people

The Solution

We built NexaCRM as a Next.js app with a PostgreSQL database. The dashboard is built around a simple drag-and-drop deal board, contact history, and automatic follow-up reminders, with no setup needed to get started.

  • A drag-and-drop board to move deals through each stage
  • A full history of every call, email and note in one place
  • Automatic reminders so deals don't go cold
  • Team can start using it within 10 minutes of signing up

The Outcome

NexaCRM launched with a simple sign-up flow, and its first small sales teams said they were fully set up in under a day, a fraction of the time they'd spent trying out bigger tools.
