Information We Collect

When you fill out a form on our website, reach out by email or phone, or otherwise get in touch with Pixelync, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and company name
  • Details you share about your project, budget or timeline
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used only to keep the site working properly

How We Use It

We use your information to answer your questions, prepare quotes, and deliver the service you asked for. If you agree, we may also send occasional updates about our work. We never use your information for automatic decisions or profiling.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with third parties where necessary to deliver a service you've requested (for example, a payment processor for an invoice), or where required by law.

Cookies

Our website may use minimal, essential cookies to remember basic preferences and understand how visitors use the site. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No way of sending data over the internet is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee complete safety.

Your Rights

You can ask us any time to see, fix, or delete the personal information we have about you. Just email us at info.pixelync@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, reach out to us at info.pixelync@gmail.com or through our contact page.
