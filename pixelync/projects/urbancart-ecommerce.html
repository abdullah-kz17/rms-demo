UrbanCart, A Faster Online Store For A Growing Shop

Pixelync built a new online store and inventory system for UrbanCart, a home goods shop that had outgrown its old template-based setup.

ClientUrbanCart Home Goods
IndustryRetail / E-Commerce
Timeline14 Weeks
ServicesWeb Dev, Cloud, UI/UX

The Challenge

UrbanCart's old template-based Shopify store couldn't keep up anymore. Pages loaded slowly on mobile, stock had to be updated by hand across two warehouses, and every new feature meant paying for another app, some of which didn't work well together.

  • More people were leaving before checkout because pages were slow
  • Updating stock by hand led to regular overselling
  • No control over checkout design or discounts

The Solution

We built a new online store from scratch, with a Node.js and PostgreSQL backend to handle products, stock and orders, and a fast React frontend. A dedicated stock system keeps inventory in sync across both warehouses in real time.

  • A fast store that loads in under 2 seconds
  • Stock stays in sync across both warehouses in real time
  • A discounts system that replaced three paid Shopify apps
  • Stripe checkout with saved payment methods

The Outcome

UrbanCart moved to the new store with zero downtime. In the first few months, more people completed their purchases on mobile, and the shop got full control over checkout and discounts again.
