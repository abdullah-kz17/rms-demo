Our Story

Built By Engineers Who Got Tired Of Bloated Agencies

Cortexa started in 2012 as a three-person freelance collective frustrated with how slow and expensive enterprise software vendors were for mid-sized businesses. Today we're a team of 80+ engineers, designers and strategists — still small enough to move fast, experienced enough to be trusted with mission-critical systems.
