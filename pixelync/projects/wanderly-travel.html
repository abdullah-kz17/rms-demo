Wanderly, A Booking Site For A Small Tour Company

Pixelync built Wanderly, a booking site that replaced spreadsheets and phone bookings for a small tour company running multi-day trips.

ClientWanderly Travel Co.
IndustryTravel / Tourism
Timeline12 Weeks
ServicesWeb Dev, Cloud, UI/UX

The Challenge

Wanderly had a growing number of multi-day tours, but every booking was still handled through email and a shared spreadsheet. Seats weren't tracked in real time, and collecting payment from a group trip meant chasing a dozen travelers by hand.

  • Trips were overbooked because seats were tracked by hand
  • No way for travelers to book or pay online themselves
  • Hours spent every week sorting out partial group payments

The Solution

We built a booking site in Vue.js backed by a Django server, with Redis holding each seat during checkout so two travelers can never book the same last spot. Stripe handles full payments and split deposits for group bookings.

  • Live seat availability with a short hold during checkout
  • Travelers can book and manage their own trip online
  • Split payments for group and family bookings
  • Automatic emails and reminders before each trip

The Outcome

Wanderly moved its whole booking process online within the first season, stopped overbooking trips, and cut the time staff spent chasing payments by hand.
