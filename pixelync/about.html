Our Story

Why We Started Pixelync

Pixelync started in Lahore in 2020 when Abdullah Khan, Abdul Hannan and Abdul Moeez decided to build the kind of studio they wished existed as clients themselves — small enough to be genuinely accountable, senior enough to be trusted with real production systems.

We work with a short list of clients at a time by design. That means every project gets direct access to the people actually writing the code and making the architecture decisions — not a account manager relaying messages to an offshore team you never talk to.
